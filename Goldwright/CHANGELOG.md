# Goldwright changelog

## 0.7.8

### New
- Added the Buy tab: look up any item by name or Alt-click it in your bags, also away from the auction house. ([#37](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/37))
- Added today's price compared with the item's usual, its sales, trend, cheapest listings and the other houses' prices to the Buy tab. ([#37](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/37))
- Added buying on the Buy tab at the auction house: Under Normal fills the quantity, Buy shows the exact price, Confirm buys. ([#37](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/37))
- Added "+ Shopping List" on the Buy tab, with a choice of list. ([#37](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/37))
- Added item names to Goldwright Data (English), so the Buy tab can find any item. ([#36](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/36))

### Improved
- Alt-clicking an item scrolls the Sell tab's bag list to it. ([#39](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/39))
- Button labels use Title Case throughout. ([#41](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/41))

### Bug Fixes
- Fixed "Internal auction error" when switching tabs while a price waited for Confirm. ([#40](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/40))
