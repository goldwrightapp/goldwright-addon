# Goldwright changelog

## 0.7.4

A new look for the Sell tab: calmer, wider, and easier to read, in two looks to choose from. ([#12](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/12))

- **Wide layout:** your bags on the left; on the right the item with three numbers (**Lowest now**, **Normal, last 7 days**, **Sold per day**; hover it for what it counts and whether it is an estimate), one wide chart with a **History / Best time to post** switch next to **Cheapest listings**, and your listing along the bottom. Each price shows once, money lines up in columns, and the numbered headers, the help lines and the searches counter are gone.
- **Your bags:** each item shows its price each, and a small tag only when something matters: "77% above normal", "vendor pays more", "Blackwater +80%". The total at the bottom is "Worth about …".
- **Your listing:** two prices to pick from, **Fast** and **Patient**, each showing just its price. Then price, quantity and duration, and **You get, after the 5% cut** with the deposit and how many units are listed cheaper than you (buyers take those first). A warning box appears only when something needs attention: a price far from normal, a vendor paying more, or a price too high to sell in time.
- **Fast matches the cheapest listing:** our hourly snapshots show that on Forever, ore, herbs, cloth and other stackable goods at the same price sell newest first. Posting at the cheapest price already puts you first in line, so going 1c under bought nothing and only pushed prices down. Fast now matches the cheapest real listing by default; Settings > Undercut by still offers 1 Copper or a percentage. **Patient** stays about 10% above the market price. Typical / Market is gone: when Fast would be more than 15% under the market price or net less than a vendor pays, Patient is chosen and the line under the buttons says why. ([#15](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/15))
- **Cheapest listings** now show a bar for each quantity and how many are listed in total (auctions for items, units for commodities), in place of the old "Listings" number that counted price rows. ([#11](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/11))
- **Hover the charts for exact values:** a thin line snaps to the nearest day or hour and a tooltip shows its numbers. On the price history: that day's market price, the normal price, and what you sold that day (days you sold on get a small gold mark). On best time to post: the hour in your time, how many are listed on average, the market price, and whether it's in the best time to post; a faint line marks the current hour. With a controller, select the chart, press A, then left and right. ([#13](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/13))
- **Market window:** click a day on the price history (or press A on it with a controller) to open a small window with five tabs, each one big answer over one chart, for the house you sell at over the same 7 or 30 days the price history shows. Nothing in it is a forecast: each tab compares today with the item's own usual. **Price:** today's price against its normal range (the middle half of the days shown, shaded): cheaper than most days, in the normal range, or higher. **Demand:** how many sell a day as bars under the price line (proven sales solid, the estimated rest lighter) and whether the last 3 days were busier or quieter than usual ([#17](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/17): needs the newer Goldwright Data with daily sales). **Supply:** how much is listed now against how much is usually listed at this hour of the day. **Resell:** buy under the low end of the normal range less the cut and you profit even reselling at the low end of normal; buy now or wait. **Your trades:** your profit after the cut, with your buys and sales on the price line. Hover any chart for exact numbers; LB and RB switch tabs. ([#16](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/16))
- **Two looks:** **Blizzard** (the default, like the game's own windows) and **Goldwright dark**, in Settings under Look. A change of look applies after /reload. ElvUI and EllesmereUI players keep their own look.
- **Controller:** LB and RB switch between the tabs.
- My Auctions, Shopping and Scan work as before in the new window; their own new look comes in a later release.
- **Fix: a very high price no longer shows a Lua error.** Typing a price like 9,999,999 gold in the Sell tab showed an "integer overflow" error; the warning now says "More than 10× its normal price" (and the bag list "over 10× normal"). The quantity box also corrects itself to what you have when you type more. A listing whose total is more than a character can hold (9,999,999 gold) can't be posted: the warning says "Too high". ([#18](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/18))
