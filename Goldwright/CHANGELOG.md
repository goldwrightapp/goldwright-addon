# Goldwright changelog

## 0.7.11

### New
- Look up gear by its full name in the Buy tab ("Medicine Staff of the Tiger"), each variant with its own prices, listings and chart. ([#58](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/58))
- Buy gear in the Buy tab and in shopping lists: Buy, then Confirm. ([#61](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/61))
- Price history charts for every item with a few days of prices, gear variants included. ([#59](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/59))

### Improved
- Gear charts load the first time you open one, so logging in stays as quick as before. ([#59](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/59))
- Look up suggestions appear instantly, even after one letter. ([#58](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/58))

### Bug Fixes
- Fixed shopping lists and the price watch treating gear variants ("of the Owl", "of the Bear") as one item. ([#60](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/60))
