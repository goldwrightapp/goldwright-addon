# Goldwright changelog

## 0.7.9

### New
- Added a Stack prices setting for bag tooltips: Whole Stack, One Item or Both. ([#44](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/44))

### Improved
- Price charts have smooth, even lines at any UI scale. ([#38](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/38))
- Price charts show a dot on each day, and the price history a faint fill under its line. ([#38](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/38))
- Small price moves now look small on the charts. ([#38](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/38))
