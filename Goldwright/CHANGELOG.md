# Goldwright changelog

## 0.7.10

### New
- Goldwright now saves full scans started by other add-ons, such as Auctionator, and counts them toward the 15 minutes. ([#51](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/51))
- Added a Plan to target on the Level up tab: 75, 150, 225 or 300. ([#31](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/31))
- Added stepping through price chart points with the controller's D-pad. ([#19](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/19))

### Improved
- Full scans stay smooth on slower computers and the Steam Deck. ([#53](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/53))
- A full scan with no answer from the server stops waiting after 60 seconds. ([#51](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/51))
- Settings notes name the key you chose for the expanded tooltip. ([#45](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/45))

### Bug Fixes
- Fixed auction sale mail sometimes being counted twice in Sales. ([#46](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/46))
- Fixed level-up plans being shared between characters with the same first name. ([#47](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/47))
- Fixed the Recipes tab showing the previous profession after switching on the Level up tab. ([#50](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/50))
- Fixed the controller's D-pad stopping at the last row on screen in Goldwright's lists. ([#57](https://github.com/goldwrightapp/goldwright-addon/issues/57))
